Who we are

6311arts is an inclusive art center founded in 2025 in a 5,000-square-foot former funeral home in Rogers Edge. Under the original wood ceiling you’ll find a gallery, a working classroom and a shop, all built to give local artists of every background a safe place to show, sell and teach.

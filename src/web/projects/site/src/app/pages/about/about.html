Our story

6311arts opened in 2025 in a 5,000-square-foot former funeral home in Rogers Edge, the corner of Edgewater where it meets Rogers Park. Under the building’s original wood ceiling we built a gallery, a classroom and a shop.

Our mission is simple: give local artists of all backgrounds a unique and safe space to exhibit and sell their work, and back it up with free career development sessions. We teach printmaking, watercolor, mosaic and more.

The gallery

Three rooms of rotating exhibitions, free and open five days a week.

The classroom

A print studio and workshop space where local artists teach small groups.

The shop

Hundreds of original works by artists who live within six miles.
